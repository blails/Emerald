WHY EMERALD IS DIFFERENT

More than a long list
of slash commands.

Emerald focuses on how server systems work together — from the first member join to moderation, support and day-to-day community activity.

See how it works ↗
01

One place for the essentials

Moderation, security, tickets, logs, roles and community activities are available from the same bot.

02

Configurable moderation

Choose AutoMod modules, sensitivity, exemptions, actions and moderator-channel reporting to fit your server.

03

Bulk cleanup for spam bursts

Spam detection can remove batches of messages together, fall back to individual deletion when needed, and route the incident through Emerald's moderation action and logging flow.

04

Clearer owner visibility

Private owner analytics show operational metrics and aggregate command usage without collecting command message contents for that counter.

05

Privacy controls built in

The /privacy panel explains stored data and lets a member confirm deletion of Emerald-managed records.
